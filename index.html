What is ANZEN?

Comprehensive AI Vehicle Safety Platform

A single integrated system addressing the critical gaps in current road safety solutions in Sri Lanka and beyond.

Real-Time Road Hazard Detection

YOLOv8 Nano on Raspberry Pi for potholes, obstacles, and accidents with GPS logging and cloud sync.

  • Edge AI processing
  • MongoDB + React Native dashboard

AI-Based Accident Detection & Response

MPU6050 + YOLOv5 + Random Forest for instant severity assessment and emergency alerts.

Multi-Modal Safety Alerts

Blind spot (RCWL-0516 radar), sharp bends, traffic sign recognition using YOLOv8 and OBD-II data.

Predictive Driver Behavior Analysis

MobileNetV3 + BiLSTM for drowsiness, distraction, and risky behavior prediction with ETP algorithm.

Our Domain

Research Foundation

Research Gap

Existing Intelligent Transportation Systems handle only discrete tasks. No unified platform combines automated hazard detection, real-time accident severity assessment, multi-modal alerts, and predictive driver behavior monitoring with sufficient forecast horizon.

01

Lack of integrated geo-fenced + radar-based blind spot detection

02

Absence of temporal multimodal predictive modeling for driver risk

The Problem

Road accidents remain a major public health crisis. Sri Lanka recorded over 2,500 fatal accidents in 2024–2025, causing thousands of deaths and severe injuries. Current systems are reactive, manual, and fragmented.

2,562 Fatal Accidents (2025)
2,710 Deaths (2025)
1.35M Global Annual Deaths
11.5 Fatalities per 100k (SL)
